Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Cric Ticker mobile app ("Cric Ticker", "the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Cric Ticker app for Android and iOS and to this website.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your name, phone number, email address or location.
  • Your app settings stay on your device.
  • To deliver scores, the app talks to our servers over an encrypted connection. Our servers see your IP address for security and to prevent abuse.
  • If the app shows ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers as described in section 5.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data on your phone so it works the way you left it:

  • your settings, such as light or dark theme;
  • recently loaded cricket data, so screens open quickly and work briefly offline;
  • counters that decide when to ask you to rate the app.

This data is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Information processed by our servers

When the app requests scores, fixtures, rankings or other cricket data, our servers receive the request together with your device's IP address and basic technical details (time of the request and the data requested). We use this only to deliver the data, keep the service secure, limit abusive or automated traffic, and fix problems. We do not use it to identify you or build a profile, and we keep server logs only for a limited period.

Requests between the app and our servers are encrypted.

4. Permissions

On Android the app uses the Internet permission and the network-state permission, to load data and show whether you are offline. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

5. Advertising

The app may display ads served by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and interactions with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Ratings and store services

If you choose to rate the app, the rating is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your account details.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with service providers that help run the app (hosting and, if ads are shown, Google AdMob), or when required by law.

8. Children

Cric Ticker is a general-audience sports app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and data deletion

Because there are no accounts, we hold no profile about you to delete. You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it. You can control ad personalisation in your device settings. For any privacy request, including access or deletion requests where a law gives you those rights, email us at the address below.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit and limit access to our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we process.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
